# Code of Conduct

We want OS Cams to be a friendly place to learn and build, for people of any background and experience.

- Be kind. Assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic and constructive. Beginners' questions are welcome.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads and block people who don't follow this. To report a problem, use a private message or the repository's private security contact.

This is a short version in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
